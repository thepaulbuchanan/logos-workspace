name: Ad Hominem / Personal Attack
tags: [fallacy, ethos-corruption, poisoning-the-well]
---
### Formal Definition
Let $P$ be a proposition asserted by agent $A$. A linguistic transition violates this lemma if it rejects or invalidates $P$ based on a negative evaluation of $A$'s personal character, identity, or traits $C(A)$, rather than addressing the truth value of $P$.

$$\forall P, A : \text{Assert}(A, P) \land \text{Attack}(C(A)) \implies \text{Violates\_SVE-L102}$$

### Rejected Human Language Syntax
* "He is so corrupt that you cannot trust his data."
* "That so-called expert has no standing to comment on this field."

### Approved Reframed Human Language Syntax
* "The structural data submitted must be evaluated on its own analytical merits, independent of the past professional disagreements of the author."
