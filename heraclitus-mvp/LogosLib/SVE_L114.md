### 2. Verification Context
114. "Scoring" (also, Moving the Ball Down the Field, the Sports World Fallacy; "Hey, Sports Fans!"): An instance of
faulty analogy, the common contemporary fallacy of inappropriately and most often offensively applying sports,
gaming, hunting or other recreational imagery to unrelated areas of life, such as war or intimacy. E.g., "Nope, I
haven't scored with Francis yet, but last night I managed to get to third base!"  or "We really need to take our ground
game into Kim's half of the ﬁeld if we ever expect to score against North Korea." This fallacy is almost always
soaked in testosterone and machismo. An associated fallacy is that of Evening up the Score (also, Getting Even),
exacting tit-for-tat vengeance as though life were some sort of "point-score" sports contest. Counter-arguments to the
"Scoring" fallacy usually fall on deaf ears, since the one and only purpose for playing a game is to "score," isn't it?
