### 2. Verification Context
122. The Snow Job (also Falacia ad Verbosium; Information Bias): A fallacy of logos, “proving” a claim by
overwhelming an audience ("snowing them under") with mountains of true but marginally-relevant  documents,
graphs, words, facts, numbers, information and statistics that look extremely impressive but which the intended
audience cannot be expected to understand or properly evaluate. This is a corrupted argument from logos. See also,
"Lying with Statistics." The opposite of this fallacy is the Plain Truth Fallacy.
