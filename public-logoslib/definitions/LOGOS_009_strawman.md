# Master Lemma: The Straw Man Fallacy

## 1. Description
Occurs when an entity systematically refutes an altered, exaggerated, or fundamentally misrepresented version of an opponent's original argument, rather than addressing the actual structural points of the thesis.

## 2. Text Example
- **Original Claim A:** "We should redirect a portion of our defense budget toward local infrastructure."
- **Distorted Target A_Prime:** "My opponent wants to completely dismantle our military and leave our nation completely defenseless."
- **Fallacy:** Attacking `A_Prime` illegally under the claim of defeating `A`.
