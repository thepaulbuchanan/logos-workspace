# Master Lemma: Hasty Generalization

## 1. Description
A faulty inductive generalization where a broad conclusion is asserted based on a sample size that is either too small or completely unrepresentative of the parent population distribution.

## 2. Text Example
- **Sample Instance S:** "My grandfather smoked four packs of cigarettes a day and lived to be 95."
- **Inference Claim:** "Therefore, smoking cigarettes cannot be harmful to human health."
- **Fallacy:** Extracting a universal rule from an isolated outlier dataset index.
