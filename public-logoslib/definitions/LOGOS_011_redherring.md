# Master Lemma: The Red Herring Fallacy

## 1. Description
An informal fallacy where an entity introduces a completely irrelevant topic or assertion into an active argument thread to divert attention away from the original proposition, concluding that the original point has been addressed or defeated.

## 2. Text Example
- **Original Claim A:** "We must upgrade our structural corporate compliance filters to stop logic leaks."
- **Extraneous Claim B:** "Why focus on compliance when our competitors are spending twice as much on standard marketing campaigns?"
- **Fallacy:** Treating the introduction of an unrelated corporate budget comparison as an implicit refutation of compliance security constraints.
