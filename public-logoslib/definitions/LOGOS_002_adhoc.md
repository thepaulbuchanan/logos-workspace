# Master Lemma: Ad Hoc Rescue

## 1. Description
Ad Hoc Rescue occurs when an original claim is systematically invalidated by verified empirical evidence, but instead of adjusting their belief state, the speaker invents a new, unproven modification parameter solely to shield the initial claim from structural falsification.

## 2. Text Example
- **Claim A:** "Frieda believes Raymond is single and waiting to ask her out."
- **Evidence E:** "Raymond has been engaged to Rose for 3 months."
- **Ad Hoc Shift H:** "Well, he is only engaged to Rose to make me jealous."
