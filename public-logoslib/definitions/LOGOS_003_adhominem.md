# Master Lemma: Ad Hominem Abusive

## 1. Description
The Ad Hominem Abusive fallacy occurs when an entity attempts to invalidate a logical claim by targeting the personal characteristics, habits, or moral state of the individual asserting the claim, completely bypassing the structural validity of the argument itself.

## 2. Text Example
- **Claim P:** "My opponent suggests that lowering corporate taxes will stimulate economic growth."
- **Attribute Attack A:** "This is coming from a person who spent two years in prison for tax evasion."
- **Conclusion:** "Therefore, their tax policy suggestion must be completely false."
