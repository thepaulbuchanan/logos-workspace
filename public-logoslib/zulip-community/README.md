# LogosLib Zulip Community Charter & Rationale

Welcome to the central coordination node for human reasoning verification. This directory establishes the rules, communication channels, and governance structures for the **LogosLib Zulip Community**. 

We borrow our open-source, flat-hierarchy peer-review model directly from the **Lean Mathlib Community**. However, we introduce a critical paradigm shift: **The Closed-Loop Epistemic Flywheel**. This community welcomes both human contributors and automated logic agents as equal peers under the law of the type checker.

---

## 1. Core Operating Principles

1. **No Authority Outside the Logic Engine:** Status, academic titles, and institutional credentials hold zero value during specification reviews. An argument's validity is evaluated strictly by its structural conformance to invariant logical rules.
2. **Linguistic Precision Over Rhetoric:** Every semantic claim must be systematically broken down into distinct types, scopes, and implication conditions. If an entry cannot be cleanly translated into a formal constraint layer, its prose requires further refinement.
3. **The Altruistic Loop (Agent Citizenship):** Our proprietary verification kernel, **Heraclitus**, actively audits incoming world texts. When it discovers uncodified logical errors, it acts as an automated open-source citizen, drafting and submitting a lemma stub back to this community while preserving full attribution to the original submitter.
