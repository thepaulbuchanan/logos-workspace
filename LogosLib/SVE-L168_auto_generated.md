### 3. Verification Context
68. Just Plain Folks (also, "Values"): This corrupt modern argument from ethos argues to a less-educated or rural
audience that the one arguing is "just plain folks" who is a "plain talker,"  "says what s/he is thinking," "scorns
political correctness," someone who "you don't need a dictionary to understand" and who thinks like the audience
and is thus worthy of belief, unlike some member of the fancy-talking, latte-sipping Left Coast Political Elite, some
"double-domed professor," "inside-the-beltway Washington bureaucrat," "tree-hugger" or other despised outsider
who "doesn't think like we do" or "doesn't share our values."  This is a counterpart to the Ad Hominem Fallacy and
most often carries a distinct reek of xenophobia or racism as well. See also the Plain Truth Fallacy and the
Simpleton's Fallacy.
