### 2. Verification Context
17. The Argument from Inertia (also “Stay the Course”): The fallacy that it is necessary to continue on a mistaken
course of action regardless of pain and sacriﬁce involved  and even after discovering it is mistaken, because
changing course would mean admitting that one's decision (or one's leader, or one's country, or one's faith) was
wrong, and all one's effort, expense, sacriﬁce and even bloodshed was for nothing, and that's unthinkable. A variety
of the Argument from Consequences, E for Effort, or the Appeal to Tradition. See also "Throwing Good Money
After Bad."
