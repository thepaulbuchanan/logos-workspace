### 3. Verification Context
78. Moving the Goalposts (also, Changing the Rules; All's Fair in Love and War; The Nuclear Option; "Winning isn't
everything, it's the only thing"): A fallacy of logos, demanding certain proof or evidence, a certain degree of support
or a certain number of votes to decide an issue, and then when this is offered, demanding even more, different or
better support in order to deny victory to an opponent. For those who practice the fallacy of Moral Superiority
(above), Moving the Goalposts is often perceived as perfectly good and permissible if necessary to prevent the
victory of Wickedness and ensure the triumph of one's own side, i.e, the Righteous.
