## 2. The Three-Pillar Epistemic Classification Matrix

### A. Epistemology (Justification & Verification Soundness)
*   **The Scope:** Audits *how* a statement claims to know a fact.
*   **Linter Checks:** Intercepts circular source referencing, appeal to unverified anonymous authority, and consensus-driven logical fallacies (`SVE-L301`).

### B. Ontology (Entity Definitions & Boundary Constraints)
*   **The Scope:** Audits *what* entities are defined and if their properties remain stable across the document history.
*   **Linter Checks:** Catches semantic variable drift (`SVE-L601`), equivocation, and corrupted cross-document references (`SVE-L401`).

### C. Phenomenology (Observational Data & Metric Invariance)
*   **The Scope:** Audits *what is observed* by cross-examining raw data, statistical balances, and mathematical formulas.
*   **Linter Checks:** Executes native background handshakes with local Lean 4/Aristotle kernels to prove equation safety and blocks stochastic timeline overreaches (`SVE-L402`).
