*   **The Open-Source License:** The grammar files, dictionary primitives, and compiler parser tokens (`Entity[...]`, `State[...]`) are released under a permissive **MIT / Apache 2.0 License**. Anyone can construct an independent downstream prover for free.
*   **The Copyright Trademark Boundary:** Poetic Technologies, Inc. retains strict international trademark and copyright control over the terms **Heraclitus Engine**, **LogosLib**, and **SVE (Semantic Validation Engine)**. 
*   **The Interoperability Lock:** Third-party engines can compile LogosLang, but they are legally and structurally barred from copying the master **`LogosLib` monorepo registry** database hashes.
