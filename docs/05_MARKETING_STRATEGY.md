## 1. The Tier-1 Open-Source Growth Flywheel (Freemium Pipeline)
Poetic bypasses expensive traditional marketing channels by deploying a **Product-Led Growth (PLG) open-source flywheel**. By distributing the Tier 1 Post-Facto Analyzer as a free download for academics, researchers, and data engineers, we establish a zero-friction adoption curve:
*   **The Land Phase:** Independent logicians use the free compiler to analyze completed documents (`.pdf`, `.tex`), popularizing the **LogosLang specification language** globally.
*   **The Community Lock:** Academics and third-party AI developers submit new rule proposals to our centralized **Zulip LogosLib Monorepo**. This group-sourced activity dynamically expands our active database lemmas at near-zero research overhead cost.
*   **The Enterprise Expand Trigger:** As non-technical corporate executives receive reports compiled with our open `ep_hash` system tokens, their legal, audit, or risk mitigation teams realize they cannot afford to compile documents *after* they are written. This friction automatically drives institutional upgrades to our premium, real-time **Tier 3 Live-Synthesis Canvas Suite**.

## 2. Unit Economic Performance Models (LTV / CAC Metrics)
Our low-overhead PLG pipeline ensures that our customer acquisition costs are suppressed, while our highly sticky enterprise subscription matrices drive massive customer lifetime value:

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Efficiency
*   **The Formula:** Because our Tier 1 open-source track handles the top-of-funnel conversion work natively, Poetic's direct enterprise sales spend is isolated strictly to executive inside sales engineers and technical account managers targeting pre-qualified inbound accounts.
*   **Target Enterprise CAC:** $32,000 per Tier 3 institutional account contract.

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) Optimization
*   **The Formula:** A Tier 3 enterprise contract requires a strict minimum contract threshold of 100 seats, priced at $650 / seat / month ($7,800 / seat / year). This locks a minimum initial baseline contract value of **$780,000 per institutional account annually**.
*   **Retention Matrix:** Because the Heraclitus real-time inline canvas becomes deeply embedded directly inside an organization's writing infrastructure (similar to Overleaf or corporate document management portals), our platform switches into an unyielding structural dependency.
*   **Target LTV Bounds:** Assuming a standard 5-year corporate contract lifetime and a minimal net revenue churn rate (< 2% annually):
    *   *Baseline Annual Contract Value (ACV):* $780,000
    *   *Lifetime Value (LTV):* $780,000 × 5 years = **$3,900,000**
*   **LTV-to-CAC Ratio:** $3,900,000 / $32,000 = **121:1 Efficiency Profile** (an exceptional margin ratio for institutional lead venture funds).
